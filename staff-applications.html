Recruitment

APPLICATION QUEUE.

Accept adds the player to the public roster and deletes the application. Deny deletes the application without adding them. A confirmation appears before either action.

Players & contacts

ROSTER CONTROL.

Emails appear only in this protected staff portal. Use the contact action to open a prefilled message. Removing a player takes them off the public roster; it does not send a message.

Partnerships & media

PARTNERSHIP QUEUE.

Inquiries from the partnerships page arrive here. Contact the requester by their own email, then delete the inquiry once it is handled. Nothing on this list is public.

Members

INVITE CODES.

Generate a one-time invite code for an accepted player. Codes are shown once, stored only as a hash, valid for 30 days, and revocable any time before they are redeemed. The redemption path unlocks when the member account system goes live.
