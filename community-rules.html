COMMUNITY RULES

Expected conduct, fair play, player safety, and reporting principles.

V

Last reviewed: October 15, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Standard

VAYNTR aims to value respect, reliability, teamwork, fair play, and improvement. These standards do not mean a staffed moderation or appeals system is already operational.

2. Expected behavior

  • Treat players, staff, opponents, organizers, and community members respectfully.
  • Give constructive feedback about play, not personal attacks.
  • Respect privacy, boundaries, and a person's choice not to engage.
  • Represent VAYNTR honestly; do not invent roles, results, sponsorships, or permissions.
  • Follow current game, platform, tournament, and community rules.

3. Prohibited behavior

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory abuse.
  • Doxxing or sharing private messages, contact details, or recordings without permission.
  • Cheating, prohibited automation, account theft, scams, malware, or service disruption.
  • Impersonation, forged evidence, fabricated results, or false affiliation claims.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, coercion, inappropriate contact with minors, or retaliation against good-faith reports.

4. Fair play and privacy

Do not use prohibited cheats or automation in competition. Never request or publish passwords, recovery codes, home addresses, school information, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive data. Staff should not request account passwords or ask members to install untrusted software.

5. Enforcement and reporting

If a moderation process is implemented, possible actions may include warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, role removal, or removal from a community. Decisions should be proportionate and based on evidence. A private VAYNTR reporting channel is not yet configured; do not post sensitive evidence in public GitHub issues. Use game/platform safety reporting tools where appropriate.

6. Review

Rules may change as VAYNTR develops. A written policy alone does not guarantee enforcement; authorized staff and secure reporting channels are necessary.
