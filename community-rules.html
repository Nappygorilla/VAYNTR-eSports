COMMUNITY RULES

Expected conduct, fair play, member account rules, and reporting principles.

V

Last reviewed: October 21, 2026. These rules apply to everyone who applies to join, interacts with the community, or holds a VAYNTR member account. Enforcement ultimately depends on authorized staff acting on evidence — this document alone is not automatic enforcement.

1. Standard

VAYNTR aims to value respect, reliability, teamwork, fair play, and improvement. These standards complement, and never replace, the game, platform, and tournament rules of wherever play happens.

2. Expected behavior

  • Treat players, staff, opponents, organizers, and community members respectfully.
  • Give constructive feedback about play, not personal attacks.
  • Respect privacy, boundaries, and a person's choice not to engage.
  • Represent VAYNTR honestly; do not invent roles, results, sponsorships, or permissions.
  • Keep your member profile accurate — your display name, game and platform details, and answers given in your application should match reality.
  • Follow current game, platform, tournament, and community rules.

3. Prohibited behavior

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory abuse.
  • Doxxing or sharing private messages, contact details, or recordings without permission.
  • Cheating, prohibited automation, account theft, scams, malware, or service disruption.
  • Impersonation, forged evidence, fabricated results, or false affiliation claims.
  • Sharing, selling, or using another person's member account, credentials, or invite code; attempts to bypass sign-in limits, rate limits, or access of any data another member's account is restricted to.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, coercion, inappropriate contact with minors, or retaliation against good-faith reports.

4. Member accounts

Member accounts are invite-gated and personal. Keep two accounts at most — one per person — never share them with another party, and report a suspected compromise to staff as soon as possible. Sessions live in your browser's session storage and end when the tab closes or when you sign out. If you sign in on a shared or public device, use it only when necessary and sign out yourself immediately afterward.

5. Fair play and privacy

Do not use prohibited cheats or automation in competition. Never request or publish passwords, recovery codes, home addresses, school information, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive data. Staff should not request account passwords or ask members to install untrusted software.

6. Enforcement and reporting

Possible actions for conduct breaking these rules include warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions, role removal, account removal, or removal from a community. Decisions should be proportionate and based on evidence. A dedicated VAYNTR reporting channel is being set up; until it is live, follow the interim reporting routes in the Privacy Policy, section 11, and never post sensitive evidence or private details in public GitHub issues. Use game/platform safety reporting tools where appropriate.

7. Review

Rules may change as VAYNTR develops. A written policy alone does not guarantee enforcement; authorized staff and secure reporting channels are necessary.
