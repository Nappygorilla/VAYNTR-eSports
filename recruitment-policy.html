RECRUITMENT & PLAYER SAFETY

Application fields, eligibility, age safeguards, retention, and recruitment standards.

V

Last reviewed: October 21, 2026. This is a practical operating policy, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Current application process

The application form on the recruitment page asks a set of structured questions grouped into sections, and it adapts to the track you pick in What are you applying for?:

  • Everyone: display name, contact email, platform, game/division, and confirmation that the answers are truthful and complete.
  • Competitive profile (player applicants): in-game handle, rank, competitive experience, proudest moment, and game details.
  • Commitment & communication: availability, time-zone region, preferred schedule window, microphone access, prior-roster status, how you give and receive feedback, why you want to join VAYNTR, and your schedule agreement.
  • Media team details (media applicants only): the skills you can contribute, the tools you use, and a single link to your single best content piece.

Applications are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in VAYNTR's Render PostgreSQL database, where they are visible only in the protected staff portal. VAYNTR stores a link to content you produced (for example YouTube or a portfolio link); it does not accept general file uploads from applicants, and it never displays applicant contact details publicly. When the operator has configured the mail service, a short confirmation email may be sent to the address you provide.

2. Eligibility and age

The Terms of Use set the eligibility standard: applicants must be at least 13 years old, and applicants under 18 must involve a parent or guardian in the process where required or appropriate. Providing a false age is grounds for decisions or membership to be voided. VAYNTR does not collect personal information from children under 13, and no independent age-verification system runs at this time. Note that individual games, platforms, and tournament organizers often enforce higher minimum ages — those requirements override anything the site permits.

3. Information and selection

The form collects only what it needs to assess fit and reach applicants: gamertag/handle, division and platform, experience, availability, time zone, and one working email address. Do not include more in free-text answers or content links — no home addresses, school details, government IDs, passwords, private account access, precise locations, or health/financial data. Extra sensitive details that slip in are deleted from the record as soon as staff notice them (see the Privacy Policy, section 10).

Staff review applications manually in the staff portal. Applying is not placement: no entitlement to a roster spot, income, sponsorship, or competition success is created. Prospective joining members should be told the expectations for activity, commitment, and conduct before they agree.

4. Decisions and communication

Staff can accept an applicant, which keeps them as a roster record, or deny them — both decisions delete the application. Accepted players are contacted manually using the email address they submitted; that address stays private and never appears on the public roster. You can ask to withdraw or correct an application before a decision by using the routes described in the Privacy Policy, section 11.

5. Safe communication

Staff should contact applicants only through the email they provided or approved team channels, and never ask applicants for passwords, account credentials, recovery codes, payments, or to install untrusted software. For minors, communication is age-appropriate and guardian involvement is encouraged. A dedicated private reporting and safeguarding channel is being set up; until it is live, follow the interim routes in the Privacy Policy, section 11, and use game/platform safety tools where a bigger platform than VAYNTR is involved.

6. Records and membership

Only authorized staff can review applications. Retention follows the Privacy Policy: applications are deleted at decision time (accepted or denied); the roster record of an accepted player is kept while they are a member and, for members, cascades delete together with their account when the member appoints a deletion request. The operator must confirm backups, follow the retention schedule, and limit staff access to those who need it. Expectations for activity, conduct, roster changes, and removal for members appear in the Community Rules and Terms of Use.

7. Launch checklist

  • Finishing the private safeguarding/reporting channel remains an open item.
  • Confirm database backups and the retention schedule are being followed in practice.
  • Get qualified legal review of the recruitment process before commercial operation or paid positions.
