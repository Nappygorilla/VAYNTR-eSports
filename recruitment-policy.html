RECRUITMENT & PLAYER SAFETY

Application status, eligibility, age-related safeguards, and recruitment standards.

V

Last reviewed: October 16, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Current application status

The recruitment page requires a direct YouTube video link explaining why the applicant wants to join VAYNTR. The form is available when the database and secure staff review key are configured; automated email sending is optional. Applications are stored in Render PostgreSQL for authorized staff review. Staff can accept an applicant to add them to the public roster and delete the application, or deny them and delete the application without adding them to the roster. An accepted player's email is retained privately for manual contact; it is never displayed on the public roster. VAYNTR stores the video link rather than uploading the video file. Do not submit sensitive personal information, and do not set the YouTube video to Private if reviewers need to watch it.

2. Eligibility and age

VAYNTR has not finalized a minimum age or eligibility standard. Before announcing recruitment as open, set clear rules accounting for game/platform age limits, tournament requirements, applicable law, and guardian permission where needed. Until child-privacy safeguards are reviewed and implemented, do not collect personal information from children under 13. Minors should involve a parent or guardian where required or appropriate.

3. Information and selection

Collect only what is needed to assess competitive fit and contact applicants. Prefer gamertag, division, platform, relevant experience, and a safe contact route. Avoid home addresses, school details, government IDs, passwords, private account access, precise location, and unnecessary health or financial data. Explain tryouts, expected commitments, timelines, and whether positions are paid or unpaid before a player agrees to join. Do not promise placement, income, sponsorship, or success.

4. Safe communication

Use approved, auditable channels. Staff should not pressure applicants to share secrets, install unknown software, provide credentials, or enter inappropriate private conversations. For minors, use age-appropriate communication and guardian involvement where appropriate. Publish a private safeguarding/reporting route before launch.

5. Records and membership

Only authorized staff can review applications. The decision controls delete applications on acceptance or denial; acceptance preserves a private roster contact record containing the player's email. The operator must set and follow a retention schedule for accepted roster/contact details and provide a deletion-request process. Limit staff access to those who need it. Publish expectations for activity, conduct, branding, tournaments, roster changes, removal, and appeals before membership goes live.

6. Launch checklist

  • Finalize age and eligibility rules, including guardian-consent procedures where required.
  • Secure application storage and restrict staff access.
  • Provide a private contact and safeguarding route.
  • Publish privacy, retention, deletion, and moderation procedures.
  • Get qualified legal review of the recruitment process.
