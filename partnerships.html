Before reaching out

BE CLEAR.
BE REAL.

Useful inquiries explain who you are, what you are proposing, and how the collaboration would work.

Include in your message

  • Your name or organization and the best way to reply.
  • The type of collaboration and the specific goal.
  • Any relevant dates, requirements, links, and deliverables.
  • For media, the original source and permission or credit requirements.

For now, use the site's contact page. Do not send passwords, private account credentials, or payment details. VAYNTR does not promise sponsorship, compensation, or tournament placement through this page.
