Make a YouTube video explaining why you want to join VAYNTR and what you would bring to the organization. Paste the direct link in the form.

An Unlisted YouTube video is fine. Do not set it to Private, because reviewers may not be able to watch it. Submit a direct video link, not a channel or playlist link. Avoid including private or sensitive personal information in the video.
