LEGAL CENTER

Policies, current feature status, privacy requests, and launch-readiness information.

V

Last reviewed: October 15, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

Current website status

VAYNTR is still in development. Recruitment submissions are not transmitted, and the member dashboard is a browser-only preview. Supabase client initialization exists on some pages, but authentication, cloud profiles, member verification, staff permissions, and production database security are not complete.

Policies

  • Privacy Policy — browser storage and third-party requests.
  • Terms of Use — acceptable use and demo limitations.
  • Community Rules — conduct, fair play, and safety.
  • Recruitment & Player Safety — eligibility, applications, and safer communication.
  • Cookies & Browser Storage — local storage and tracking practices.
  • Copyright & Intellectual Property — VAYNTR branding and third-party materials.
  • Disclaimer — independence and competitive advice limitations.

Before production launch

Do not enable real applications or member accounts until the backend has Row Level Security, server-side authorization, trusted membership verification, account recovery, deletion workflows, and incident response. Publish a monitored private contact address and have a qualified legal professional review these policies.

Contact and privacy requests

A dedicated private legal/privacy contact is not configured. For non-sensitive issues, use the VAYNTR website repository. Public GitHub issues are not appropriate for private requests. Never post personal information, identity documents, passwords, or confidential evidence there. Set up a monitored private channel before collecting real data.

Important

Publishing a policy does not itself make a website compliant or secure. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, user age, data collected, and whether VAYNTR operates commercially.
