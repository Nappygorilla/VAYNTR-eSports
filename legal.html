LEGAL CENTER

Policies, current feature status, privacy requests, and launch-readiness information.

V

Last reviewed: October 21, 2026. This page summarizes the current state of the site and its policies. It is not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance.

Current website status

VAYNTR is an independent esports project in active development. The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages; the recruitment API and PostgreSQL database run on Render. The recruitment form collects a structured application (competitive profile, commitment, and — for media applicants — a media details section) for staff review. Staff review applications through the protected staff portal and contact applicants manually using the email they provided; accepted players appear on the public roster while their contact email never does. Member accounts are live and invite-gated: staff issue one-time invite codes, members register, sign in with optional multi-factor authentication, and manage their profile and picture in the member portal. Recruitment and member features may be temporarily unavailable while the database or API is not ready.

Policies

  • Privacy Policy — personal data VAYNTR collects (applications, member accounts, security logging), retention, and how to request changes.
  • Terms of Use — eligibility, application decisions, account responsibilities, and acceptable use.
  • Community Rules — conduct, fair play, member account rules, and reporting principles.
  • Recruitment & Player Safety — eligibility, application fields, decisions, and safer communication.
  • Cookies & Browser Storage — cookies (none), local storage, and where sign-in sessions live.
  • Copyright & Intellectual Property — VAYNTR branding, member uploads, and third-party materials.
  • Disclaimer — independence and competitive advice limitations.

What is already operational

  • Applications and member data are stored in Render PostgreSQL, reachable only over TLS-verified connections.
  • Failed sign-ins and access attempts are rate-limited, with escalating lockouts; IP addresses in security logs are stored only as truncated SHA-256 hashes.
  • Member passwords are stored as scrypt hashes; MFA secrets are stored encrypted; staff and member sessions use session-storage tokens validated server-side.
  • Public roster responses are validated server-side so contact emails cannot leak.
  • Member avatars are validated by file signature and capped to 300 KB before storage.

Still outstanding before broader launch

  • A monitored private privacy/legal request channel (the interim route is below).
  • Confirmation that database backups and the retention schedule are being followed in practice.
  • Decide which markets recruitment is open in and verify the corresponding minimum-age and guardian-consent rules for those markets; the application's parent/guardian checkbox is a self-declaration, not independently verified consent.
  • A formal notice-and-takedown process for intellectual property matters.
  • Qualified legal review of these policies, especially before any commercial operation, paid positions, or sponsored content.

Contact and privacy requests

A dedicated private legal/privacy contact is being set up. Until it exists: members can raise most profile changes directly in the member portal; for anything else, use non-public routes described in the Privacy Policy, section 11; as a last resort for non-sensitive issues only, use the VAYNTR website repository. Do not post personal information, identity documents, passwords, invite codes, or confidential evidence in public GitHub issues.

Important

Publishing a policy does not itself make a website compliant or secure. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, user age, data collected, and whether VAYNTR operates commercially.
