Recruitment / what to expect

THE PATH
TO VAYNTR.

A straightforward process built around clear information, a manual review, and honest decisions. Submitting an application does not guarantee a roster spot.

V

Four steps

FROM APPLICATION
TO A DECISION.

Every application uses the official recruitment page. Public account registration is not part of the current process.

01 / PREPARE01

Choose your division

Pick the game and platform you actually play, and review the competitive standards before applying.

02 / APPLY02

Submit the form

Provide your gaming name, contact email, platform, division, and a direct YouTube video link explaining your interest.

03 / REVIEW03

Staff review

Applications are reviewed manually. Keep your video Public or Unlisted so reviewers with the link can open it; do not use Private visibility.

04 / DECISION04

Receive a decision

Staff will contact the email address supplied in the form. No response deadline is promised, and a decision should never be assumed from silence.

IMPORTANT / PRIVACY!

Share carefully

Do not include passwords, home addresses, school details, or other sensitive information in your application or video. Read the Privacy Policy.
