COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE

Current local storage, third-party requests, and future tracking changes.

V

Last reviewed: October 15, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Current technologies

VAYNTR does not intentionally run an advertising tracker or dedicated analytics platform at the time of review. Settings and dashboard demos use browser local storage, which is not a server account and can be cleared in browser site-data settings.

2. Local storage keys

  • vayntr-settings-profile — settings demo values.
  • vayntr-member-dashboard-profile — dashboard preview profile/settings.

These values are not synced to a verified account and may be visible to others using the same browser profile.

3. Third-party requests

Pages may request Google Fonts. Pages loading the main JavaScript may initialize the Supabase browser client and request its library from a third-party delivery host. Hosting and service providers may process technical request information under their own policies.

4. Consent and future changes

No consent banner is shown because the site does not intentionally run advertising or analytics tracking at the time of review. This is not a legal conclusion that consent is never required. Before adding analytics, advertising, marketing pixels, or embedded media, assess applicable law, update this notice, and implement required consent controls.

5. Managing storage

Remove local storage through browser site settings or clear the site's browsing data. Blocking Google Fonts may change appearance.
