COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE

Current cookies, browser storage, sign-in sessions, and third-party requests.

V

Last reviewed: October 21, 2026. This is a practical summary of how the site works today, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Cookies

VAYNTR sets no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies — the site currently sets no cookies of its own at all. Anything described below is stored in your browser only and is never synced to a server-side advertising profile.

2. Browser storage the site uses today

  • vayntr-settings-profile (local storage) — the on-page settings demo preferences. Local storage persists until you clear it; it is not a server account and can be visible to others using the same browser profile.
  • Member sign-in sessions (session storage) — when you sign in through the member portal, the session token is kept in your browser's session storage and sent only to VAYNTR's own API as a login credential. Session storage is automatically cleared when you close the tab or browser window, and signing out clears it explicitly. VAYNTR does not store your session token in cookies or persistent local storage.
  • Staff portal sessions (session storage) — the protected staff dashboard also keeps its access token in session storage only, cleared on sign-out or when the tab closes.

3. Third-party requests

Pages may request Google Fonts. Recruitment submissions, the public roster, member sign-in, profile and avatar management, and the staff portal all connect to VAYNTR's Render API over HTTPS. Google Fonts and Render may process technical request information (such as IP addresses) under their own policies; neither is used to track you across other sites.

4. Consent and future changes

No consent banner is shown because the site does not run advertising or analytics tracking at the time of review. This is not a legal conclusion that consent is never required: if analytics, advertising, marketing pixels, or embedded media are added, applicable law will need to be assessed and this notice updated with the required consent controls before launch.

5. Managing storage

Clear local or session data through your browser's site-data settings. Signing out of the member portal immediately clears your session token. Blocking Google Fonts may change the site's appearance but not its functionality.
