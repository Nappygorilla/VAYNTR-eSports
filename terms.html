Last reviewed: October 15, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. About VAYNTR

VAYNTR is an independent esports project. These terms cover its informational website and demos; they do not represent that VAYNTR is a registered company, professional league, publisher partner, or active commercial service.

2. Demo features

The recruitment page remains paused until database, verified sender, and secure staff review configuration is ready. When open, the form transmits application details and a direct YouTube video link to the Render API for staff review and sends transactional emails through Resend. VAYNTR stores the link, not the video file. Public account registration is disabled by default; the website does not issue membership automatically.

Do not break the law, harass or threaten others, impersonate people or organizations, interfere with availability, attempt unauthorized access, distribute malicious code, scrape private information, or infringe rights. Do not bypass security controls or exploit unfinished features.

No production account or membership entitlement is issued through this website. A code entered into the demo is not proof of membership. Account security, suspension, appeals, recovery, and data terms must be published before account features launch.

Publish only content you have rights and permission to use. Do not share another person's personal information or private communications without permission. 7. Informational content

The site is provided on an “as available” basis to the extent permitted by law. Nothing here excludes rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded or limited.

The site may be updated, paused, or discontinued. Governing law and dispute venue are not specified because the operator's legal status and intended jurisdiction have not been confirmed; seek qualified legal review before commercial launch. For non-sensitive site issues, use the VAYNTR website repository. Do not post private information publicly.
