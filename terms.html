TERMS OF USE

Rules for using the VAYNTR website: browsing, applying for the roster, and member account responsibilities.

V

Last reviewed: October 20, 2026. These terms describe how the site actually works today. They are a practical starting point, not legal advice and not a guarantee of enforceability in every jurisdiction; have them reviewed by a qualified professional before any commercial use.

1. About VAYNTR

VAYNTR is an independent amateur esports project. These terms cover its informational website, the recruitment application, and the invite-gated member portal. They do not represent that VAYNTR is a registered company, a professional league, a publisher partner, or a commercial service, and they do not promise prizes, salaries, or any competitive outcome.

2. Who can use the site

  • You must be at least 13 years old to submit an application or hold a member account.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must know about and permit your participation, including the details you submit and your videos. VAYNTR relies on your confirmation of this — see the Privacy Policy for what that means.
  • You may browse all public pages regardless of age, subject to these terms.

3. What the site provides today

  • Public website: roster, divisions, news ("Intel"), results, media library, recruitment information, and legal policies.
  • Recruitment application: open when VAYNTR's backend reports it is ready; applications go to staff review only. Contact after review is manual.
  • Member portal: accepted players and media members can sign in with staff-issued one-time invite codes, with optional multi-factor authentication, a self-managed profile picture, a public bio, and age-visibility controls.

The staff portal is a separate restricted area; access to it is not part of these public terms and is granted only to VAYNTR's own operators.

4. Applications and recruitment decisions

Submitting an application is not a contract of employment or membership, and no roster spot is reserved by applying. Applications are reviewed by VAYNTR staff using the details you submitted and the Recruitment & Player Safety standards. By applying you agree that:

  • All answers must be accurate and your own. Materially false answers — including a false age claim — are grounds to decline or void a membership later without warning.
  • VAYNTR may accept, decline, waitlist, or pause applications at its discretion, and is not required to give reasons.
  • Acceptance is communicated manually when it happens; VAYNTR currently sends no automated decisions.
  • Declining does not obligate VAYNTR to delete your device history with the browser's stored values or answers you later resubmit; erasure requests follow the Privacy Policy.

5. Member account responsibilities

If you are accepted and create a member account, you agree to:

  • Use only the account VAYNTR issued for you, one account per person, and never redeem a code meant for someone else.
  • Keep your password and multi-factor secrets private, sign out of shared computers, and tell staff promptly if you believe your account was accessed by someone else.
  • Upload only a profile picture you have the right to use, and keep your public bio and links lawful and appropriate.
  • Follow staff instructions on roster information; VAYNTR may correct, hide, or remove profile content it considers inaccurate or unsafe.
  • Accept that VAYNTR may suspend or revoke an account and remove a player from the public roster for rule-breaking, inactivity agreed with you, inactivity without notice to the roster, or at the operator's discretion. Account suspension and appeal questions can be raised by contacting staff.

6. Content you provide

You keep the rights to content you create (application answers, your video, your bio, social links, uploaded images). You give VAYNTR surgical permission — solely as needed to operate the site — to store, display, edit for length or safety, and publish that content on roster pages, player profiles, and review areas while you are a member or applicant, and to keep published roster-profile content reasonably in sync with your edits. This permission ends when your account and roster record are deleted, except where the website cached a copy briefly during storage cleanup. Everything else about data handling is in the Privacy Policy.

7. Rules of conduct and fair play

Do not break the law, harass or threaten others, impersonate people or organizations, dox, spam the application form or staff, interfere with availability, attempt unauthorized access, distribute malicious code, scrape private information, bypass security controls, exploit unfinished features, deceive VAYNTR about your abilities or identity, or infringe rights. VAYNTR does not endorse cheating, prohibited automation, account theft, or harassment, and players must follow the governing rules of their games, platforms, and any tournaments they enter. See the Community Rules for the shared expectations.

8. Third-party links and services

Links to GitHub, Render, Google Fonts, YouTube, the Warzone Performance Lab, game publishers, and other services are provided for convenience. VAYNTR does not control those services and is not responsible for their content, availability, or practices.

9. Informational content

Game settings, loadouts, audio guidance, training plans, and performance information are general educational material. They may become outdated or may not suit a particular setup, and no rank, win rate, tournament placement, income, or competitive outcome is guaranteed.

10. Availability and changes

The site and its features are provided "as is" and "as available", with free-tier hosting; features may be paused, changed, or discontinued, including the application form or member sign-in, without individual notice. Nothing here limits any rights that cannot legally be limited.

11. Disclaimers and liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, VAYNTR and its operator are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses or lost competitive opportunities arising from the use or unavailability of the site. This is an amateur, non-commercial project run by volunteers; these terms may need revision before VAYNTR ever operates as a business, and no part of them overrides mandatory local law. Governing law and dispute venue are not fixed here because the operator's legal status has not been confirmed; ask for qualified legal advice before any contractual activity.

12. Questions and changes

These terms may be updated as features change — the "Last reviewed" date reflects the current version. For non-sensitive site issues, use the VAYNTR website repository. Do not post private information or account details publicly.
