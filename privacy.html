PRIVACY POLICY

How VAYNTR's website, recruitment applications, roster data, and third-party services handle information.

V

Last reviewed: October 16, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. Current operator and status

VAYNTR is an independent esports project using a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. This site does not identify a registered business entity or publish a dedicated private privacy-request email.

2. Data entered into demos

The recruitment form is designed to collect a gaming username/display name, email address, platform, game/division, a direct YouTube video URL, an optional message, and a record that the applicant acknowledged this policy. The form is available when the database and secure staff review are configured. Applicant contact is currently handled manually; automated confirmation and decision emails are not enabled. Submitted applications are stored in the Render PostgreSQL database and can be viewed only through authorized staff routes. When staff accept an applicant, the application is deleted after their display name, email address, game/division, and platform are copied into a private roster record. The accepted player's email remains private for manual contact. When staff deny an applicant, the application is deleted without adding them to the roster. Public roster pages can show a player’s display name, game/division, platform, role, bio, social and highlight links, and optional profile image, depending on what staff publishes. Contact email addresses are kept out of public roster responses. VAYNTR stores the YouTube URL, not the video file; YouTube processes and hosts the video under its own terms and privacy practices. An Unlisted video is viewable by anyone who has its link, so do not include private or sensitive information. The settings demo may save values in browser local storage under vayntr-settings-profile; these values are not synced to an account and may be accessible to others using the same browser profile.

3. Hosting and third-party services

GitHub Pages serves the public website. Render hosts the recruitment API and PostgreSQL database. VAYNTR currently handles applicant contact manually. If automated messages are enabled in the future, Resend may process those messages; this is not currently enabled. Applicants' YouTube videos remain hosted by YouTube; VAYNTR stores only the submitted URL. Google Fonts requests font files from Google. Third-party providers process technical or message-delivery data under their own privacy terms, and external links have their own practices.

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

VAYNTR does not intentionally run advertising trackers or a dedicated analytics product at the time of review. The demos use browser local storage. Revisit this statement before adding analytics, ads, marketing pixels, embedded media, or other tracking. See Cookies & Browser Storage.

5. Security and minimization

Do not submit sensitive information in your application or video. Application data is intended to be available only to authorized staff through server-side protected routes. VAYNTR must verify that database credentials, staff access keys, backups, access controls, incident response, retention, deletion, and applicable consent procedures are properly configured before recruitment opens. Secret API keys and database credentials must never be placed in browser code.

6. Children and younger players

The current demo is not designed to collect children's personal information. Do not submit personal information if you are under 13. Before knowingly collecting information from children or recruiting younger players, VAYNTR must assess applicable child-privacy and consent requirements and implement suitable safeguards.

7. Choices and requests

Clear local values through your browser's site-data settings. Blocking Google Fonts may change the site's appearance. A dedicated private privacy-request channel is not configured. Do not post personal data, passwords, identity documents, or confidential evidence in public GitHub issues. A monitored private contact method and deletion-request process must be configured before recruitment is announced as open.

8. Retention and changes

Pending application records are deleted when staff accept or deny the application. For accepted players, the roster retains their display name, email address for private manual contact, game/division, platform, and acceptance timestamp until the player is removed or the record is otherwise deleted by the operator. The operator should establish and follow a retention/deletion schedule for roster contact details. Browser demo values remain until cleared or removed by the browser.

9. Contact

For non-sensitive site issues, use the VAYNTR website repository. Do not post private information publicly.
