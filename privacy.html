PRIVACY POLICY

How VAYNTR's website, recruitment applications, member accounts, roster data, and third-party services handle information.

V

Last reviewed: October 20, 2026. This policy describes what the website actually does today. It is a practical starting point, not legal advice and not a guarantee of compliance; review it against applicable law in your jurisdiction before relying on it.

1. Who we are

VAYNTR is an independent amateur esports project operated through a static website hosted on GitHub Pages and a small API with a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render. VAYNTR is not a registered company, league, or publisher partner. This policy covers information collected through the public website, the recruitment application, the invite-gated member portal, and VAYNTR's own server logs. It does not cover the practices of third-party services discussed in section 7.

2. What VAYNTR collects, at a glance

  • Visitors (you are now): nothing beyond what your browser and hosting provider handle technically. No cookies are set, no analytics run, and no advertising attempts deliver on the site.
  • Applicants: the details you type into the recruitment form, recorded and stored only on VAYNTR's own backend for staff review.
  • Members: roster-profile data, a hashed password, session records, optional multi-factor authentication, the profile picture you choose to upload, and your public bio.
  • Security records: login, lockout, and MFA events kept for monitoring, with IP addresses reduced to truncated SHA-256 hashes before they are stored.

VAYNTR never asks for information the site does not need, never sells data, and gives applicants only public-facing information to recruiters.

3. Recruitment applications

When the application form is open, submitting it collects and stores the following in VAYNTR's Render PostgreSQL database:

  • Contact: gaming username/display name and your email address.
  • Who you are as a player: your age, whether you allow that age on your public profile, platform, the division you are applying for, your in-game handle or account ID, your current rank or competitive level, and a description of your competitive experience.
  • Optional context: up to three social media links, a competitive achievement you are proud of, division-specific details such as a preferred drop, loadout, or camera settings, and your best scheduling window.
  • Commitment: the hours per week you can commit, your time zone or region, whether you have a working microphone, how you handle feedback, and why you want to join VAYNTR in particular.
  • History: whether you have applied to VAYNTR before or are currently on another team's roster.
  • If you are applying for the media team: your editing/recording tools, the skills you bring, and a link to your single best content piece. Media applicants do not submit a gameplay video.
  • Player track: a direct YouTube URL showing why you want to join VAYNTR. VAYNTR stores the link only, never the video; your video stays on YouTube under their own terms.
  • Anything else you choose to add: an optional free-text notes field.
  • Records of your consents: that you confirmed you are 13 or older (and, if under 18, that a parent or guardian permits the application), and that you consented to this policy's use of your details.

All answers are read only by authorized VAYNTR staff through a server-side protected route. Applications are never public, never consumed by analytics, and never copied anywhere outside VAYNTR's own database. If staff accept an application, the application record is deleted after your display name, email, game/division, platform, age, and age-visibility choice are copied into your private roster record. If staff decline or a decision is made to erase your details, the application is deleted without creating a roster record. Applicant contact is handled manually; VAYNTR does not currently send automated confirmation or decision emails.

4. Member accounts

Accepted players and media members may create an account producing the features on the member portal:

  • Invite codes: staff generate a single-use, random invite code meant only for you. Codes expire 30 days after creation and are revoked once used. VAYNTR stores the code's hash, not the code itself, so the plaintext cannot be recovered from the database.
  • Passwords: passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes and are never displayed, emailed, or readable in website code. VAYNTR requires strong passwords.
  • Sessions: signing in stores a random token in your browser's session storage; the backend keeps only a hash. Signing out or closing your browser clears your access. Expired session records are cleaned up automatically. Any public member actions (e.g., posting roster data) require a live session of your own rather than shared site data.
  • Multi-factor authentication (optional): once enrollment is complete, the account is protected by a time-based authenticator-app code. VAYNTR stores the secret encrypted server-side; nothing is retained in the browser. Those challenge rows are deleted once used or expired.
  • Profile picture: you may upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image up to 300 KB. It is stored inside VAYNTR's own database (not a cloud file host) and appears only where your roster profile is shown (roster cards and your player profile). You can remove it at any time.
  • Public bio and age visibility: you can write or edit a short bio (up to 500 characters) and choose whether your recorded age appears on your public profile. Staff can change the age-visibility setting on request at any time.

5. Security and automated abuse controls

VAYNTR protects account data with layered controls: request rate limits, a client-solved proof-of-work once repeated sign-in failures look automated, progressive lockouts with increasing delays, and database connections that verify their server's TLS certificate by default. Failed sign-ins, lockouts, and MFA verification events are recorded for staff monitoring. These records show when an event happened, a human-readable event type and any name hint you had entered before the failure, and your IP address only after it was reduced to a truncated SHA-256 hash — the full address is never written to the database. Only authorized staff can read these records; they are never published. Your site is served from an isolated infrastructure container and VAYNTR staff keys and secrets are never put into website code.

VAYNTR keeps a scheduled workflow (not personal-data related) that pings its own uptime endpoint so the free hosting tier stays warm. That workflow transmits no personal data.

6. Public roster information

Public pages may show an accepted player's display name, game/division, platform, role and bio, public social or highlight links, the profile image the player chose (either their uploaded picture or a staff-set image URL), and the recorded age — but the age leaves the database only when the player explicitly consented to showing it, and the choice can be changed on request at any time. Contact email addresses are never sent to public responses. Public roster query results are validated server-side so none of your private contact details can leak.

7. Hosting and third-party services

  • GitHub Pages serves the public website's static files.
  • Render hosts the recruitment/member API and the PostgreSQL database, in a U.S. region, with connections encrypted by TLS.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's font files directly from Google's CDN (your browser requests them from Google's servers).
  • YouTube hosts the videos applicants link; VAYNTR never sees or stores the video files themselves, only the submitted URL.
  • Resend is the provider VAYNTR would use if automated email notifications are enabled in the future. This is not currently enabled.

Third-party providers process technical or message-delivery data under their own privacy terms, and external links have their own practices. Reviewing those policies is your responsibility before using linked services.

8. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

VAYNTR runs no cookies, no ad trackers, and no analytics product. There are no advertising pixels. Members see their sign-in token kept in browser session storage (cleared on tab close); a small demo settings key (vayntr-settings-profile) may save display preferences in your browser's local storage and is not synced to any account. See Cookies & Browser Storage for the precise current list.

9. Children and younger players

The application form requires a minimum age of 13, and the applicant must confirm that, if they are under 18, a parent or guardian permits them to apply. So that young people can join a team safely:

  • People under 13 are not accepted and must not submit personal details.
  • Applicants under 18 should have a parent or guardian read this policy and the Recruitment & Player Safety rules before they apply, and are expected not to submit sensitive details (addresses, identity documents, private accounts, medical or school information) in answers or videos.
  • VAYNTR is an independent amateur project and does not currently verify ages or parental consent documents; it relies on the applicant's confirmation and reserves the right to decline applications when details seem inconsistent, especially where minors apply.

10. Minimization

Do not submit anything in your application or video that the site does not request and does not need — full addresses, phone numbers, payment or identity details, medical or school information, or images of documents. If VAYNTR ever receives something like that submitter information unknowingly, staff will delete it from the record as soon as it is noticed. Uploads are capped to images (≤300 KB) instead of allowing general file attachments.

11. Your choices and requests

Members control most public profile details themselves through the member portal, including uploading or removing their picture, editing their bio, deciding whether their age shows, and managing their opt-in for photos from their sign-in sessions. Any member can ask staff to delete their account and roster record together. Applicants and other people may ask VAYNTR to delete an application, request a copy of what was submitted, fix details, or withdraw an application before a decision.

A dedicated privacy-request channel is being set up. Until it exists: members can raise most changes directly in the member portal; for anything else, first try the non-public reviewer appropriate (see the Contact page); as a last resort, you can open a GitHub issue that does not include any private details. Do not post passwords, addresses, identification, or confidential application evidence in public issues.

12. Retention and changes

Pending application records are deleted once staff make a decision (accepted or declined). For accepted players, the roster retains the records described in section 6 until the player is removed or the record is deleted by the operator. If the player creates a member account, the account's name, its salted password hash, its active sessions, its MFA configuration, the member-uploaded profile picture, and the bios/age settings are all deleted together when the roster record is deleted at removal time. VAYNTR's operator should set and follow a documented retention/deletion schedule for roster contact details, and remains data-controller risk for anything it keeps longer. Browser demo values stay until cleared or removed by your browser.

VAYNTR may update this policy as features change — check the "Last reviewed" date. Material changes affecting existing applicants or members will be clearly announced on the site rather than quietly rewritten.

13. Contact

For non-sensitive site issues, use the VAYNTR website repository. Do not post private information publicly. Sensitive member requests should be raised privately with staff once a monitored private channel is live (this policy's section 11 describes how).
