Team culture / conduct

COMPETE WITH
INTENT.

Competitive ambition only works when teammates can trust each other. These standards outline the baseline expected of applicants and players representing VAYNTR.

V

The baseline

HOW WE
REPRESENT.

Read these alongside the Community Rules and Recruitment & Player Safety policy. Role- and event-specific requirements may add detail.

01 / FAIR PLAY01

Win honestly

Do not cheat, exploit prohibited tools, manipulate competitive records, or misrepresent performance. Results shared as official should be accurate and supportable.

02 / RESPECT02

Respect people

No harassment, threats, hate-based abuse, doxxing, or targeted humiliation of teammates, opponents, staff, or community members.

03 / COMMUNICATION03

Keep comms useful

Communicate clearly, give teammates room to speak, and handle disagreement without personal attacks.

04 / RELIABILITY04

Be honest about availability

Give reasonable notice when you cannot attend a planned team activity. Do not claim a commitment you cannot reliably make.

05 / REPRESENTATION05

Protect the reputation

Use approved assets and accurate team affiliation. Do not announce partnerships, sponsors, roster spots, or results without authorization.

06 / SAFETY06

Respect privacy

Keep account credentials and private contact information private. Do not pressure players to share personal details, passwords, or off-platform information.

Expectations before applying

TEAM FIRST.
NO SHORTCUTS.

We are building the organization step by step. Applicants should understand the standards before asking to represent the team.
