COPYRIGHT & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

How VAYNTR approaches its own branding and third-party game content.

V

Last reviewed: October 15, 2026. This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review it against actual operations and applicable law before production launch.

1. VAYNTR materials

Unless a specific license or credit says otherwise, VAYNTR intends its original name, logo, graphics, written content, and site design to remain with their respective rights holders. Do not use VAYNTR branding in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation without permission.

2. Third-party intellectual property

Call of Duty, Warzone, Rocket League, platform names, publisher logos, game footage, music, screenshots, and other materials may be protected by copyright, trademark, contract, or platform policies. Their owners retain their rights. Mentioning a game does not imply ownership, sponsorship, or permission to use protected assets.

3. Content permissions

Before publishing a player's clip, photograph, logo, artwork, or profile details, obtain the permissions needed for the intended use and confirm that third-party assets are allowed. Do not submit content you do not have authority to use. The site currently has no public upload system.

4. Infringement concerns

A dedicated private legal contact and formal notice process are not configured. Do not post private data or confidential evidence in public GitHub issues. Before hosting user-generated content or operating commercially, establish a monitored private process and seek advice about formal notice-and-takedown obligations that apply.

5. No false affiliation

Do not falsely claim that VAYNTR owns a game, publisher, league, sponsor, or another team's mark, or that a third party endorses VAYNTR. Partnership claims should be accurate and authorized.

6. Scope

This is a general policy statement, not a formal notice-and-takedown procedure or legal advice. Applicable rights and processes vary by jurisdiction.
